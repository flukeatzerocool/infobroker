Before any encryption leg, the probe asserts the resolved key source is the synthetic file or variable it set. A probe that would write to a real path is `NOT_RUN` with the reason. **Encryption findings are advisory-only for in-session fixing if the fix would move key material or change the on-disk format** — that is a migration, not a bounded fix; it lands as an R6 register row (P1, `Scheduled-roadmap`) with a D57 note only for any R2/R8-side surface.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Secret and store blast radius.** Deep depth and D53's isolation are the mitigation; no probe touches a real key or store, and format/key-layout changes are scheduled rather than improvised.
- **Gate-versus-AAR ordering.** Mitigated by the step 0 stub (EV12); the finished AAR replaces it at step 24 without a token gap, and step 25 re-runs the fast suite over the finished tree.
- **Split triggers.** Mitigated by D50's definitions and the recorded Phase 1 count; the pre-scan (~87 anchors) is close enough to 120 that the enumeration is the authority. The split never advances the program past the review.
- **Thin citation coverage.** Several clause-bearing REQs rely on a joint header citation (`kb.test.ts:1`, `kb-sqlite.test.ts:1`); D52's evidence-class rule stops a static reading from being recorded as a tested `holds`.
- **Shared modules.** `corroborate.ts` (R5), `config.ts` (R2), `content-policy.ts` (R8), and `report-*` (R7) are evidence only; D57 keeps their defects out of the R6 register.
- **P0 overrun.** A P0 exceeding D50 stops the chunk with `LOOP OPEN`.
- **Test isolation.** The KB tests already run against temp stores; a test that writes to the shipped path would be a P0 and is covered by `test-isolation.test.ts` plus `check-shipped-kb-empty`.
