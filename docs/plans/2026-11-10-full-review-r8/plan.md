## Risks and mitigations

- **State and credential blast radius.** Deep depth plus D72's isolation is the mitigation; the probe sets its own `HOME`, `TMPDIR`, `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, `XDG_DATA_HOME`, `XDG_STATE_HOME`, and `INFOBROKER_CONFIG` and clears ambient secrets, so an accidental default path resolves inside the probe root. Any escape is a P0 that halts the chunk before Phase 3.
- **Baseline repair scope creep and weak evidence.** The repair is one file and one concern; its acceptance rests on three consecutive full-suite runs and a recorded duration, not on the isolated run that already passed pre-fix. It never edits a test's assertions to make a failure disappear.
- **Split triggers.** Mitigated by D69's recorded definitions; the pre-scan (126 reference lines; probe legs on A, B, E, F) means a split may well trigger, but only step 11's per-cluster test-observation rule decides. The split never advances the program past the review.
- **Gate-versus-AAR ordering.** Mitigated by the step 0 stub; step 27 runs the full suite over the finished tree.
- **Network-dependent verdicts.** `REQ-091` registry parity and the live `REQ-112` fetch may be unservable; D73 records `NOT_RUN` and leans on the deterministic gates rather than treating the absence as a pass or a failure.
- **Probe legs that the shipped code cannot support.** The rename registry is empty and the update check has no injectable fetcher *at the server's call sites*; step 12 states the reachable behavior for each leg, keeps the update-check leg offline through the cache short-circuit rather than a stub, and pins the unreachable transform to `src/config-migrations.test.ts` instead of pretending it is live.
- **Permission assertions on a permissive filesystem.** A container or umask can mask a `0600` defect. The probe records the observed mode and the process umask, and a leg that cannot distinguish the two is `NOT_RUN` with that reason instead of a false `holds`.
- **Shared modules.** `config.ts` (R2), `kb-sqlite.ts`/`kb-crypto.ts` (R6), and `error-envelope.ts` (R1) are partly evidence only; D77 keeps their defects out of the R8 register.
- **P0 overrun.** A P0 exceeding D69 stops the chunk with `LOOP OPEN`.
- **Unpushed backlog.** 21 commits predate this increment (EV13); they are not folded into this plan (C-1). A failure caused by that stale base is diagnosed from `git log` before any R8 finding is recorded.
